# Satla catalog and salesman ordering site

This directory is where the site for catalog.satlaspirits.com gets built. Stages 1 (sign in, catalog, product page) and 2 (customers, order history, invoice) are live as of 2026-11-14. Stage 3 (New Order, Copy Order, the order email) and an Admin page for logins are live as of 2026-11-15, and admins edit, add and delete catalog items on the site since 2026-11-16, and order emails are SENT since 2026-11-17 (see `docs/HANDOFF-2026-11-17.md`, "The order email"; `manage.py order-email off` switches sending off).

## What is here
- `catalog_web.py`: the Flask site. Reads only `Catalog.db`; never calls QuickBooks or MMK. An order is never stored: it lives in the browser (`static/order.js`) until Send, which emails it through the library's `mail_send.send_mail()`. Every form posted by a signed-in person carries a `csrf` token (`form_token()` in templates).
- `catalog_db.py`: Catalog.db schema and queries (products, users with an is_admin flag and a list of allowed states, live_snapshot per state, and qbo_customers / qbo_invoices / qbo_last_prices: a read-only copy of QuickBooks customers, 12 months of invoices, and what each customer last paid for each product from all invoices, replaced by the refresh timer).
- `catalog_live.py`: the read-only QuickBooks / MMK reads, all through the library in `~/python/qbo_scripts`. It loops over the library's `STATES` table; nothing in the site names a state.
- `catalog_items.py`: editing the catalog's items (edit, add, delete, undo, photos, the change history, rewriting the catalog file). Every change to a product goes through its `save()`.
- `catalog_source.py`: the retailer side of the edit form: reads a product page on kosherwineworld.com, ranks that store's product list for the Find button, and reads a KosherWine.com page an admin pasted (see "Find and paste"). The only outbound web requests the site makes besides the refresh: Kosher Wine World pages and pictures, and KosherWine.com pictures.
- `manage.py`: init, load-products (needs --force), export-products, load-pack, refresh, store-list, add-user, set-password, remove-user, list-users, set-admin, set-states, order-email. Run with `~/python/venvs/qbo311/bin/python3 /home/dovid/python/catalog/manage.py <command>`.
- `templates/`, `static/`: pages, styles and scripts (taken from the approved wireframes). `static/common.js` holds the helpers every page script shares (escaping, search, photo box, money); do not copy them into a page script.
- `photos/grid`, `photos/detail`: 423 photos each (2026-11-17), named `<SKU>.webp` (photos taken out of the site are kept in `photos/removed-2026-11-16/` and `photos/replaced-2026-11-15/`).
- Counts as of 2026-11-17 (after his evening answers, see "His answers, the evening of 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-17"): Catalog.db 425 products (423 with a photo; `I-SR04-R` and `I-SR08-R` have none); Illinois lists 183; Florida lists 457 (347 catalog products, 110 with no catalog entry). The rest of this line is the count before those answers.
- Counts as of the evening of 2026-11-16: Catalog.db 408 products (178 in the morning, then 2 added by him, 124 by the first list and 104 by the second); Illinois lists 178; Florida lists 457 (331 catalog products, 126 with no catalog entry). Older counts further down are as they stood on the day they were written.
- `deploy/`: service, refresh timer, Caddy site and `install.sh`. Service `catalog-web` on 127.0.0.1:5001; timer `catalog-refresh` every 30 minutes. After changing Python code or templates: `sudo systemctl restart catalog-web`.
- No new packages: it runs on the existing `qbo311` environment. Do not `pip install` into it.

## Rules of the release, in brief
- Salesmen only. Illinois and Florida are kept completely apart (see "Illinois and Florida" below): a switch in the header picks the state, and each login has the states it may use.
- Kosher labels (the owner's words, 2026-11-15): "Everything we sell is Kosher. Please just have the Mevushal symbol. Also, please don't have a box saying "Kosher for Passover". Instead, make that a category filter only". No "Kosher" or "Kosher for Passover" label on a card or product page; only Mevushal (and Organic). Kosher for Passover is a menu category and a filter. "Everything except the liqour / sake is Kosher for Passover all year round. I still feel that it should be a filter, but just so you know, all wines should show up, only Alcohol should be considered Not default." (2026-11-15) Every Wine product counts as Kosher for Passover (`_kfp()` in `catalog_web.py`); other products only when the product data says Yes. "RTD - NOT Kosher for Passover" (2026-11-16): the 4 Ready-to-Drink products are not; that is how the site already behaves.
- Country filter (the owner's words, 2026-11-15): "on the filters by country, I don't need a lower-level filter by region. Country is enough." The Filters panel has "Country" with no region list under it. Search still matches the region and the product page still shows it (he spoke only of the filter).
- No prices on the catalog grid. Regular price and cases on hand appear on the product page and the order screen.
- Orders are in cases only; part of a case is a decimal such as 0.5. No loose bottles.
- Order price default: the customer's previous price for that product, otherwise the regular price. The salesman can type over it; a drop-down offers Regular and Previous.
- The order is emailed to the address of the customer's state (since 2026-11-17: Florida to florida@satlaspirits.com, Illinois to Yoel; `order_email` in the library's `STATES`) with a copy to the salesman. It is not saved.
- Everything inside the wireframes other than the products, descriptions and photos is invented sample data.
