Satla wireframes: current release

Nine clickable screens for the three functions in functional spec v0.11: product catalog, customer lookup with order history, and salesman ordering from the customer page. Layout and rules only; this is not the final look.

Products and descriptions are real (Satla_Master_Catalog.csv, 178 products). Photos are the standardized set from the photo job: one white 3:4 frame, 600 x 800. Dotted amber marks invented sample data: customers, invoices, prices, bottles per case, salesmen.
Some product photos could not be loaded, so they show as crossed placeholder boxes. The photo files belong in a photos folder beside this page.
