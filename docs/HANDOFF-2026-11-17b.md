## Lessons from today (also in CLAUDE.md)
- **Look for the producer's own shop first.** Its Shopify `products.json` lists everything. The research missed golanispirit.com because it searched for the name "Master's Choice", which that site never uses. His words: "Totally confused how you couldn't find this???? WOW".
- **When a source leaves a field blank, check the same brand's catalog entries before leaving it blank.** His words: "ABV 40%, should be in our SYSTEM, no?"
- **Do the searching; do not hand him items to look up.** A list to approve is the form that works: he answers "all" or "all except".
- **The web-search limit is shared by all agents in a session.**
  - Size research batches to it.
  - Match against the store lists first, which costs no searches.
  - Have agents save after every item.
- **Picture fixes before the photo tool:**
  - A picture on a grey or black background: whiten the background first.
  - A phone screenshot: crop away the clock and the bars first.
  - A splash or badges under the bottle: cut at the first blank row under the bottle.
  - Check every photo on a contact sheet before saving it.
